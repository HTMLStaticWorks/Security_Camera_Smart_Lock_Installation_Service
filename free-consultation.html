Frequently Asked Questions

How long does the free consultation take?

An initial phone consultation takes about 15-20 minutes. If an on-site survey is requested, it typically takes 30-45 minutes depending on the size of the property.

Do I have to commit to buying after the consultation?

Not at all! Our consultation and quotes are 100% free and carry absolutely no obligation to purchase.

Can you integrate new cameras with my existing system?

In many cases, yes. Our technicians will assess your current DVR/NVR and wiring during the site survey to determine compatibility.

How quickly can you install the system after approval?

Most residential installations can be scheduled within 24 to 48 hours of quote approval. Larger commercial jobs may require a week's lead time for hardware procurement.
